## Things to keep in mind (NCERT's own differing figures and words)

- **People depending on agriculture:** about 75% at independence; 67.5% (1950) → 64.9% (1990) in the text; 72.1% → 66.8% (1950-51 → 1990-91) in a workforce table; '65%' in Exercise 15.
- **Services' share of GDP by 1990:** 40.59% (Box 2.4) vs 40.5 (table, 1990-91). **Agriculture in 1950-51:** 'more than 50%' (Box 2.4) vs 59.0 (table).
- **Marketed surplus** (text: what farmers actually sell) vs **marketable surplus** (Exercise 5 and a Work These Out box).
- **Land reforms:** 'change in the ownership of landholdings' (text) vs 'improvements in agriculture to increase productivity' (Exercise 19).
- **Third goal:** 'self-reliance' (text) vs 'self-sufficiency' (Recap). **The mixed economy:** 'best features of socialism without its drawbacks' (text) vs 'of socialism and capitalism' (Recap).
- **Small-scale industry limit:** NCERT says ₹5 lakh in 1950 and ₹1 crore 'at present'. Today's MSME limits are different and higher.
