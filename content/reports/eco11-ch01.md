## Things to keep in mind

- NCERT says railways were introduced in **1850**. The first passenger train (Bombay–Thane) ran in **1853**. Know both.
- NCERT says the first census was in **1881**. A non-synchronous count was taken in 1872. For NCERT-based questions, answer 1881.
- NCERT prints the 1894 Viceroy's name as 'Victor Alexander **Vruce**'. He is Victor Alexander Bruce, Lord Elgin.
- Don't confuse **R.C. Dutt** (author of 'Economic History of India') with **R.C. Desai** (national income estimator).
