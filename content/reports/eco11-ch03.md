## Things to keep in mind (NCERT vs other sources)

- **Disinvestment, 1991-92:** NCERT says the government could "mobilise ₹3,040 crore more than the target" (target ₹2,500 crore). Read it as ₹3,040 crore raised in total, which is above the target. Official figures give about ₹3,038 crore.
- **GATT:** NCERT writes "General Agreement on Trade and Tariff"; the official name is the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade. NCERT gives 1948 (when it came into force); it was signed in 1947.
- **Navratnas:** NCERT's examples are HAL, MTNL and IRCTC. HAL became a Maharatna in October 2024; use NCERT's list for NCERT questions.
- **The rupee in 1991:** devaluation "set the tone" for freeing the rupee from government control. It didn't float the rupee in 1991.
- **Industry after 1991:** the text says industry growth "fluctuated", the Recap says it "has gone down".
- **Services vs GDP:** NCERT says services grew faster than overall GDP over 2007-22, but its Table 3.1 shows services 9.2 vs total 9.4 for 2021-22 (and the 2021-22 sector figures are GVA).
- **USA textile quotas** (NCERT's non-tariff-barrier example) ended in 2005, when the WTO textile agreement expired.
- **Siricilla** has been in Telangana since 2014 (NCERT: Andhra Pradesh). **Anantpur** (Mahadeva) is still in Andhra Pradesh.
- **Disinvestment budgets:** since the 2024-25 interim budget, there's no separate disinvestment target. **GVA base year:** revised to 2022-23 in February 2026.
- **Box 3.1 in this reprint** has a garbled layout; the reel follows the PDF line by line.
